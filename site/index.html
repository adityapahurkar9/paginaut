Paginaut

A free, open-source PDF reader and editor for Windows and Linux, with an offline reader for Android.

Download Source code on GitHub
  • Free and open source (AGPL)
  • No ads, no account, no tracking
  • Works offline

Everything you do with a PDF, in one app

Paginaut opens big documents fast and keeps your files on your computer. The tools sit in a pane beside the page, so the page stays in view while you work.

Read

Smooth scrolling and zoom, two-page and book views, Read mode that reflows text to fit the window, dark pages, and themes.

Annotate

Highlight, underline, notes, text boxes, shapes, drawings and stamps, saved into the PDF so other readers see them.

Fill and sign

Fill in forms, add your handwritten or typed signature, or sign digitally with a certificate or USB token, with a trusted timestamp.

Redact and clean up

Black out text and areas for good, find phone numbers and ID numbers to mark, and remove hidden data, scripts and metadata.

Organise pages

Reorder, rotate, delete, insert, merge and split; crop and resize pages; add page numbers, headers and watermarks.

Text recognition

Make scanned documents searchable and selectable, in English, Hindi and Marathi out of the box and many more languages on request.

Convert

Save as Word, Excel (tables), Markdown, pictures or PDF/A for archiving; turn Word, Excel and PowerPoint files into PDF.

Compare and read aloud

See what changed between two versions of a document, and have any document read to you with natural voices that run on your PC.

Opens more than PDF

One reader for documents, books and comics.

  • PDF
  • EPUB
  • FB2 and MOBI e-books
  • CBZ and CBR comics
  • XPS and OXPS
  • Markdown
  • Word, Excel and PowerPoint (converted)
  • Pictures

Download

Paginaut is published only on its GitHub releases page and on Google Play. A copy from anywhere else may have been changed.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Installs for your user account, no administrator needed, and keeps itself up to date.

Windows installer

Linux

A .deb package for Debian and Ubuntu, or an AppImage for other distributions. 64-bit.

.deb and AppImage

Android

A reader for PDF, e-books and comics, with no internet access at all. In testing on Google Play; APKs are on GitHub.

Android APKs

Private by design

Your documents never leave your device. The desktop app goes online only for what you ask of it (downloading an extra language or voice) and, if you leave it on, a daily check for a new version. The Android app has no internet access. Read the privacy policy.
